Methods, definitions & sources

Official statewide NAICS series reflect taxpayer-account postings and may be revised. They can differ from Treasury cash-deposit figures. The Sept 2026 retail-subsector total differs slightly from the retail industry total; both are preserved as published. Estimates of the dollar change vs year ago are reconstructed from published rounded percentage changes and are therefore approximate. The classifications 'business / industrial' and 'consumer-facing' are initial analytical groupings, not official data fields.
